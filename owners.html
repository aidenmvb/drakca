Which buildings do you take?

Apartment houses, small mixed-use buildings, retail rows, modest office, and the occasional single house. We work in the District of Columbia, suburban Maryland, and Northern Virginia. We do not take scattered condominiums across the country.

What does management cost?

It depends on the building: size, age of the systems, and whether it is occupied. We quote after the walk-through. We do not publish a rate that ignores the property.

Can you also sell it?

Yes. Brokerage sits with management. If you want a sale, we say so in the scope. We represent buildings we know, including ones we already manage.
